Упражнение 6b

Чтение словосочетаний и предложений

💡 Нажми на ЛЮБОЕ слово в тексте, чтобы увидеть его перевод и транскрипцию!

✨
word — перевод
1. begin — begins

begin the day, begin the book. His school begins at nine. When does the film begin?

2. breakfast — breakfasts

a good breakfast, a hot breakfast, for breakfast, at breakfast, cook breakfast, have breakfast. When do you usually have breakfast? What does she usually cook for breakfast? Tom always has breakfast at eight o'clock in the morning.

3. dress — dresses

dress well, dress in the morning. Jane dresses in her room. Betty is little, she can't dress.

4. finish — finishes

finish the book, finish the text. Ann, finish your breakfast, please. When do you usually finish your work?

5. get up — gets up

get up in the morning, get up and dress. Jane doesn't get up at six. She gets up at 7 o'clock in the morning.

6. home

my home, go home, be at home. Where is your home? My home is in London. When are you usually at home? It's 8 o'clock. Go home!

7. lunch — lunches

a big lunch, a cold lunch, cook lunch, have lunch, for lunch. Rob never has lunch. Where do you usually have lunch? When we go to the zoo, we usually have our cold lunches with us.

8. be on time

Please be on time! He is never on time, but she is always on time.
